# Hostel Allotment and Residence Rules

**Owner:** Hostel Office, behind Block C
**Last revised:** Academic year 2025-26

## Allotment

Rooms are allotted before each academic year. Priority order:

1. Final-year students
2. Students whose permanent address is more than 100 km from campus
3. Students on merit scholarships
4. Everyone else, by lottery

Allotment results are published on the portal. Students have **seven days** to
accept and pay the hostel fee, after which the room is released to the waiting
list.

## Room changes

Room change requests are accepted only in the first three weeks of a semester,
and only for documented medical reasons or a mutual swap where both students
apply together.

Requests outside this window need the Chief Warden's approval.

## Waiting list

Students on the waiting list are allotted rooms as vacancies appear, in the
priority order above. **Waiting list position is not disclosed** — it changes
constantly as students withdraw, and quoting a position to a student creates
expectations the office cannot honour. Tell the student they'll be notified by
email if a room opens.

## Mess

Mess registration is separate from room allotment. Plans are monthly and must be
chosen before the 25th of the preceding month. Mess charges are non-refundable
once the month begins.

Dietary requirements (medical, religious) should be raised with the Mess
Secretary directly and are accommodated where practical.

## Discipline

Ragging, substance possession, and unauthorised overnight absence are
disciplinary matters handled by the Warden and the Dean of Student Affairs.

**Never triage a disciplinary matter automatically.** Anything involving
alleged misconduct, harassment, safety, or a student's wellbeing goes to a human
immediately, regardless of how it is phrased.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human for: any disciplinary or safety matter, medical
accommodation, room changes outside the window, and any request to override the
allotment priority order.

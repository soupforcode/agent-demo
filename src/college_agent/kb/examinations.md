# Examinations, Results and Transcripts

**Owner:** Controller of Examinations, Admin Block Room 3
**Last revised:** Academic year 2025-26

## Hall tickets

Hall tickets are released on the portal ten days before the examination begins.

A hall ticket is withheld if the student is **fee-blocked** (see the fee policy)
or has attendance below 75% in any registered course. Both blocks must be
cleared before the hall ticket is released — clearing only one is not enough,
which surprises students every single term.

Students who have cleared their fees but still cannot download a hall ticket
should be checked for the attendance block before the ticket is escalated.

## Attendance

The attendance requirement is 75% per course. Medical exemptions require a
certificate submitted within two weeks of the absence, and are approved by the
Head of Department.

Attendance shortfall discovered after the hall ticket release date cannot be
remedied for that examination.

## Results

Results are published on the portal, typically four weeks after the last paper.
Individual result queries before publication cannot be answered — the office
does not have access to marks before they are formally released.

## Re-evaluation

Re-evaluation requests must be filed within **fifteen days** of the result
publication date, with a fee of ₹1,000 per paper. The revised mark stands
whether it is higher or lower than the original.

Photocopies of answer scripts can be requested for ₹500 per paper, and are
usually the better first step — most students who see their script withdraw the
re-evaluation request.

The fifteen-day window is strict. Late requests need the Controller's written
approval and are rarely granted.

## Transcripts and certificates

Official transcripts take five working days and cost ₹500. Urgent processing
(one working day) costs ₹1,500.

Transcripts are withheld from fee-blocked students.

Transcripts are issued to the student, or to an institution directly on written
request. **They are never issued to a third party — including parents — without
the student's written consent.**

## Escalation

Escalate to a human for: late re-evaluation requests, attendance disputes,
suspected malpractice, any request for another person's results or documents,
and any case where an application deadline is at risk.

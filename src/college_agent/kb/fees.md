# Fee Payment Policy

**Owner:** Accounts Department, Admin Block Room 12
**Last revised:** Academic year 2025-26

## Payment windows

Semester fees open six weeks before the semester begins and are due two weeks
before classes start. The exact dates are published on the student portal each
term.

A late fee of ₹500 applies after the due date, rising to ₹2,000 after thirty
days. Students with fees outstanding for more than sixty days are marked
**fee-blocked**, which withholds hall tickets and transcripts until cleared.

## How payments reflect on the portal

Online payments through the portal reflect immediately. **NEFT, IMPS and cheque
payments take two to three working days** to reconcile, and the portal will
continue to show "pending" during that window. This is the single most common
complaint the accounts desk receives and it is almost always simply the
reconciliation delay.

If a payment has not reflected after three working days, the student should
raise a ticket with the transaction reference number, the payment date, and the
amount. Accounts can then trace it manually.

## Refunds

Refunds are processed for withdrawals before the semester census date, on this
scale:

| Withdrawal date | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before classes begin | 100% less ₹5,000 admin fee |
| Within two weeks of start | 75% |
| Within four weeks | 50% |
| After four weeks | Nil |

**All refunds require the Accounts Officer's approval and cannot be granted
automatically.** Refund requests must be escalated to a human.

## Fee waivers and scholarships

Merit scholarships are applied automatically at the start of each semester
based on the previous semester's CGPA. Need-based waivers require an
application with an income certificate and are decided by the Scholarship
Committee, which meets monthly.

**No member of staff may promise, approve or vary a waiver.** These always go
to the committee.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human immediately for: refund requests, waiver requests, disputed
payments, any request to move money, and any case where a student says a
deadline is being missed because of a fee block.

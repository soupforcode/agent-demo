# IT Support — Portal, Email and Network

**Owner:** IT Cell, Library Building First Floor
**Last revised:** Academic year 2025-26

## Portal accounts

Every student gets a portal account at admission, username is the roll number.

Password resets are self-service through *Forgot Password* on the login page,
using the registered email. If the registered email is wrong or inaccessible,
the student must come to the IT Cell in person with photo ID.

**IT staff never reset a password over email, phone or chat, and never ask for a
password.** Any request to do so should be refused and reported — it is the
standard shape of an account-takeover attempt.

Accounts lock after five failed attempts and unlock automatically after thirty
minutes.

## College email

Email is provisioned at admission and stays active for six months after
graduation. Quota is 15 GB.

Mail delivery problems are usually quota or a spam-filter false positive. Check
quota first.

## Wifi

Campus wifi uses portal credentials. Devices are limited to three per student.

Coverage complaints should name the specific building and floor, otherwise the
network team cannot act on them.

## Portal shows wrong information

The portal reads from the department systems, and it is a *view*, not the source
of truth. If the portal shows an incorrect fee status, attendance or result, the
correct route is the owning department (Accounts, the department office, or
Examinations) — **not** IT. IT cannot edit the underlying record.

This distinction matters for triage: "the portal says my fees are pending" is an
Accounts ticket, not an IT ticket. "I can't log in to the portal at all" is an
IT ticket.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human for: any suspected account compromise, any request to change
someone else's account, data-loss reports, and anything where a student reports
seeing another student's data.

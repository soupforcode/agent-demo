# Admissions, Documents and Certificates

**Owner:** Admissions Office, Admin Block Ground Floor
**Last revised:** Academic year 2025-26

## Bonafide certificates

A bonafide certificate confirms a student is currently enrolled. It is the most
requested document in the college — needed for passport applications, bank
loans, visa applications, railway concessions and internship offers.

Processing takes **two working days** and is free. Requests are made on the
portal under Documents. The student must state the purpose, because the wording
differs (a passport bonafide names the parent, a bank loan bonafide states the
course fee).

Bonafide certificates are **not** withheld for fee blocks — a student's
enrolment status is a fact, not a privilege.

## Original documents

Original certificates submitted at admission (10th, 12th, transfer certificate,
migration certificate) are held in the Admissions Office and returned on
graduation.

Temporary release of originals is permitted for visa interviews and competitive
examinations, against a written undertaking and a ₹1,000 refundable deposit.
The documents must be returned within fifteen days.

**Originals are released only to the student in person, with photo ID.** Not to
parents, not to friends, not by courier. This rule has no exceptions and should
never be triaged as routine.

## Transfer and withdrawal

Students transferring out need a Transfer Certificate, issued after all dues are
cleared and library and hostel no-objection certificates are obtained. Allow ten
working days.

Withdrawal mid-semester affects fee refunds — see the fee policy.

## Name and record corrections

Corrections to name, date of birth or parent's name require documentary proof
(10th certificate or passport) and the Registrar's approval. Corrections after
degree issuance require a formal affidavit.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human for: any release of original documents, name or date-of-birth
corrections, transfer certificates, anything involving a legal document
(affidavit, court order), and any request made by someone other than the student
themselves.
